How it works

Straight from Telegram
to your TV.

VueGram is built on TDLib, Telegram's own library. Each video is handed to the player by a tiny server inside the app that only answers on the TV's own address, 127.0.0.1.

Install

On your TV
in three steps.

For Android TV and Google TV, Android 6.0 or newer.

  1. 1

    Install from the TV

    Get Downloader from your TV's Play Store, type vuegram.com/get in it and choose Install. VueGram keeps itself up to date from then on.

  2. 2

    Send your API keys

    Get your free API ID and hash at my.telegram.org, then scan the TV's QR code and paste them on your phone. No typing with the remote.

  3. 3

    Scan and watch

    Scan the QR code on the TV with Telegram on your phone. Your channels appear.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

More in the docs.

Is VueGram an official Telegram app?

No. VueGram is an unofficial client built on Telegram's own library, TDLib. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram.

Does it cost anything?

No. VueGram is free and open source under the MIT license.

Why do I need my own API ID and hash?

Every app that talks to Telegram identifies itself with an API ID and hash. VueGram asks for your own, free from my.telegram.org, so your copy talks to Telegram as itself. How to get them.

Why log in with a QR code?

It needs no SMS and no login email. Telegram sometimes asks for a paid SMS or a login email before it sends a code to a phone number; the QR code avoids both.

Do videos fill up my TV's storage?

No. Videos are streamed, and the parts you watch are deleted afterwards.

A video won't play. What now?

VueGram's player tries other decoders by itself. For the rare file none of them can play, install mpv-android: VueGram then opens the video there, at the same position. See troubleshooting.

Is it on the Play Store?

No. VueGram is installed from its GitHub releases, and after that it updates itself.
