### VueGram

*Say it "view-gram".* Your Telegram channels, on the big screen.

</div>

**VueGram** is a free, open-source Android TV and Google TV app. It shows the Telegram groups and channels you follow
as a grid of their photos, and plays their videos on the TV, streamed straight from Telegram through a small server
inside the app. No bots, no third-party servers, no analytics.

- **[vuegram.com](https://vuegram.com)**: what it is
- **[docs.vuegram.com](https://docs.vuegram.com)**: install, log in, use it
- **[Releases](https://github.com/vuegram/vuegram/releases)**: download the APK
- **[vuegram/vuegram](https://github.com/vuegram/vuegram)**: the source (Kotlin, built on TDLib)
