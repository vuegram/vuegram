# Settings

Open **Settings** from the button at the top of the library (press Up from the first row of chats to reach the
header). Up and Down move between rows, OK changes or opens one, and Back returns to the library.

Out of the box VueGram shows your **groups and channels**, and only their **videos**. Two switches widen that. Both
are **off** by default, and both are stored on the TV only.

## Show people

**Off (default):** the library lists only groups and channels.

**On:** the library also lists your private chats with people, and your Saved Messages. Bots are never listed.

The library reloads by itself when you go back to it.

## Show messages

**Off (default):** a chat shows only its videos, as a grid.

**On:** a chat shows all its messages, newest first, as a list: text, photos, videos and files. In groups, each
message shows who sent it.

- **OK on a video** plays it, like in the grid. Hold OK for the same menu (mpv, from the beginning, watched).
- **OK on a photo** opens it full screen. Left and Right move to the chat's other photos; Back closes it.
- **OK on a text message** shows the whole text.
- The search field searches every message in the chat, not only videos.

The switch applies to the next chat you open.

::: tip
Turning a switch off again brings back the default view. Nothing is changed in Telegram either way.
:::

## Play next

**On (default):** when a video ends, the chat's next video starts after a 10-second countdown.

**Off:** the player closes at the end of a video.

## Language

VueGram speaks **English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Arabic,
Hebrew, Hindi, Indonesian, Persian and Turkish**. By default it follows the TV's language; pick another one here and
VueGram restarts in it. Arabic, Hebrew and Persian read right to left: lists, headers and menus are mirrored. The player's timeline and its clock stay left to
right, as on every TV player, and the arrow keys still jump back (left) and forward (right).

On Android 13 and newer you can also choose VueGram's language in the TV's own settings (Apps › VueGram › Language).

## Automatic updates

**On (default):** new versions download in the background and VueGram asks once to install them.

**Off:** VueGram only tells you a new version is out, and downloads it when you choose **Update**. See
[Updates](./updates).

## Check for updates

Asks GitHub for a newer VueGram right away, instead of waiting for the automatic check. If an update is already
downloaded, it offers **Install now**.

## Last crash report

Shown only if VueGram closed unexpectedly. OK shows what happened: the VueGram and Android versions, the TV model
and the technical details. It stays on the TV; VueGram never sends it anywhere. If you report a problem, you can copy
it into your report, or delete it from here.

## Log out

Signs this TV out of Telegram. VueGram asks first, then starts again at the login screen. Your watch history,
pinned and hidden chats and settings stay on the TV; to use VueGram again, scan a new QR code.
